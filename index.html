GOOD TO KNOW

Before you travel.

Sikkim's high-altitude and protected areas can have route and permit requirements. Current government rules should always be checked before travel.

Can you create a custom Sikkim itinerary?

Yes. Share your dates, group size, preferred destinations and budget and we'll help structure the trip.

Can you arrange Nathula Pass?

Nathula is a permit-controlled destination for eligible domestic travellers and access can change with weather and government rules. Contact us for the current process.

Can I visit North Sikkim and Gurudongmar?

These routes are permit-controlled and subject to eligibility, road conditions and weather. Contact us for a current itinerary check.

Do you arrange hotels and transportation?

Yes. Public business listings describe tour operations, transportation, accommodation and travel support.

How do I leave a review?

Use the “Leave a Google Review” button in the Reviews section. It opens Google's review flow for the Sawaaden listing.

How do I enquire?

Use WhatsApp or call +91 97755 52239. You can also visit the Gangtok office.
