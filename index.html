GOOD TO KNOW

Before you travel.

Sikkim's high-altitude and protected areas can have route and permit requirements. We'll help you plan around the current rules.

Can you create a custom Sikkim itinerary?

Yes. Share your dates, group size, preferred destinations and budget and we'll help structure the trip.

Do you arrange hotels and transportation?

Yes. Sawaaden's listed services include tour operations, tourist information, accommodation and travel arrangements.

Can I visit North Sikkim and the Silk Route?

Routes can be subject to permits, weather and road conditions. Contact us for a current itinerary and eligibility check.
